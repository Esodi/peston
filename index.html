About Peston Group

Peston Group, founded by Petro Estomih Nkya in 2016, began as a small agribusiness trading house with Peston Enterprises Limited as its flagship. From these humble beginnings, we’ve rapidly expanded into diverse sectors, building a dynamic group known for excellence and innovation.

Our growth is fuelled by a commitment to delivering quality, affordable products and services that empower clients and consumers across Tanzania and the Pan-African region.
